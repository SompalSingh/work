At Avantika, our Career Services team guides students beyond academics, fostering entrepreneurship through design and technology. We offer interactive sessions, workshops, and portfolio reviews to empower career success. Collaborating with global organizations, we facilitate internships and placements. Contact us for more on student profiles.

Career Services is dedicated to student development in particular and the University in general. It also takes care of overall student engagement and experience.
