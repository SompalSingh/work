Academic Framework

The management program at Avantika is based on the ancient Indian philosophy of PURUSHARTHA blended with contemporary skill-set requirements of Industry and society. The term Purushartha literally signifies the AIM OF LIFE or Object of Human Pursuit. The program is divided in 4-segments of Dharma, Arth, Kaam and Moksha.

DHARMA (FOUNDATION)

Critical and Creative Thinking, Introduction to Design, Sustainability, Psychology (Who am I?)

ARTH (BUSINESS CORE)

Business Essentials, Market and Economics, Organizational Behavior, Quantitative Research, Strategic Management and Panchatantra

KAAM (SPECIALIZATION, ELECTIVES AND VOCATIONAL SKILLS)

Bitcoin and Crypto Currency, Marketing Lab, Supply Chain Sustainability, Data Strategy, Marketing, Finance, Human Resource, Business Analytics, International Business, Supply Chain Management

MOKSHA (PROJECTS)

Experiential Learning, Business Model Development, Functional Business Problems, Sustainable Solutions, Industrial Training, Capstone Project
