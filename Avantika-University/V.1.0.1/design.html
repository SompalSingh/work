Industrial Design

Product Design

A 4- year UG specialisation that offers a deeper understanding of the diverse topics of product and industrial design.

Transportation Design

A 4- year UG specialization that offers a deeper understanding of the creative and critical learning of the challenges around mobility.

Communication Design

Communication Design

A 4- year UG specialization that offers a deeper understanding of the theories and concepts of graphic design and communication design.

Animation Design

A 4- year UG specialisation that offers a deeper understanding of creating special visual effects in 2D and 3D techniques for various media forms.

User Interaction and Experience Design

UI/ UX Design

A 4- year UG specialisation that offers a deeper understanding of designing user interfaces for a system or product, with the intent of enhancing the usability and the user experience.

Immersive Media Design

A 4- year UG specialization that offers a deeper understanding of Innovative design, limitless imagination – shaping the future of immersive media

Information Design

A 4- year UG specialisation that offers a deeper understanding of Information Design program

Spatial Design

Exhibition Design

A 4- year UG specialisation that offers a deeper understanding of the diverse topics of exhibition design.

Retail Space Design

A 4- year UG specialization that offers a deeper understanding of the Retail Design.

Interior Space Design

A 4- year UG specialisation that offers a deeper understanding of the creative construction of interior spaces, furniture, and the correct depiction of a concept.

Fashion Design

Fashion Design

A 4- year UG specialization that offers a deeper understanding of technical design skills, creativity, and sustainable practices to create beautiful, responsibly produced fashion.

Fashion Communication Design

Fashion Communication Design combines elements of graphic design, visual merchandising, editorial design, advertising, and styling to effectively convey fashion concepts and messages.

Apparel Design

Apparel design is a discipline that focuses on the design, development, and production of garments and clothing accessories, combining creative design skills with technical knowledge.
