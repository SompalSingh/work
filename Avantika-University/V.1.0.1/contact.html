How are you reaching us?

The nearest airport is Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport (IDR) in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, well-connected to major Indian cities. The university is 58 km (36 miles) from the airport.

DIRECTION : Indore Airport (Devi Ahillyabai Holkar Airport) => Sri Aurobindo Hospital, Bhawrasla => Sanwer => Nanakheda Bus Stand, Ujjain => Chintaman Ganesh Temple => Avantika University Campus, Lekoda.

Ujjain and Indore stations link to Delhi, Mumbai, and other cities. UJJ JN is 13 km from Avantika University, and INDB JN is 57 km away.

DIRECTION : Indore Railway Station => Ujjain Railway Station => Chintaman Ganesh Temple => Avantika University Campus, Lekoda.

Direct and transit buses from major cities like Delhi, Jaipur, Mumbai, and Pune arrive at Ujjain. The bus station is located 13 km (8 miles) from Avantika University campus.

DIRECTION : Sarwate Bus Stand, Indore => Nanakheda Bus Stand, Ujjain => Chintaman Ganesh Temple => Avantika University Campus, Lekoda.
