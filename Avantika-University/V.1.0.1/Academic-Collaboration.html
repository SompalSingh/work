Academic Collaboration

Avantika forms strategic partnerships with global institutions, enriching curriculum, research, and administrative activities.,

Global University

These collaborations enable us to build our global footprint through student exchange and faculty exchange programs, joint research ventures, joint conferences, and curriculum design.

Industry

These partnerships facilitate setting-up the Design-Tech Labs to co-create unique and impactful products or solutions, industry exposure, and domain expertise in fields of design, technology, business, society, culture, and arts to create a unique educational experience.

Academic

These alliances enable us to integrate industry-recognized certifications or courses into the curriculum and prepare our students with the critical skills and expertise they need for the 21st-century workplace.

Professional Body

These associations with member-supported organisations open the door to exciting opportunities to network with other professionals (locally and globally) aligned with our interests and to stay on top of emerging trends and innovate.

Other Collaboration

The academic programs at Avantika are led by Design, Innovation, Creativity, and Enterprise, with interdisciplinary curriculum and experimental learning at the core.
